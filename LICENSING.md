# Licensing guide

Samsarix Field Atlas uses a dual-track licensing model.

## Public license

The repository is available under the [GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 only](LICENSE), identified by the SPDX expression `AGPL-3.0-only`.

The AGPL permits commercial use, copying, modification, and redistribution under its conditions. In particular, a modified version used to provide network interaction must offer its corresponding source to those users as required by section 13. Preserve applicable copyright and license notices.

The AGPL applies to Samsarix-authored repository content unless a file says otherwise. Third-party dependencies and assets keep their own licenses.

## Commercial licensing

Samsarix LLC can discuss a separate commercial license for organizations that need to keep a modified version closed, combine the work under incompatible terms, or negotiate support, warranty, or other business terms.

Contact [contact@samsarix.com](mailto:contact@samsarix.com). A commercial exception exists only in a separate written agreement signed by the relevant copyright holders or their authorized representative; this guide does not itself grant one.

## Contributor rights

Dual licensing works only when the commercial licensor has the necessary rights. External contributors retain their copyright unless they sign a separate agreement. Samsarix LLC should use a lawyer-reviewed contributor agreement before merging substantial outside contributions that it may need to include in a separately licensed release.

The 2025 authorship notice is preserved alongside Samsarix LLC's 2026 notice. The company should keep a written intellectual-property assignment or license covering pre-company work before relying on the commercial path.

## Trademarks

Software rights and brand rights are separate. The AGPL does not grant rights to the Samsarix name, logos, or other distinctive brand assets. See [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md).

This file summarizes the intended structure for maintainers and adopters. The full license text and any signed commercial agreement control. It is not legal advice.
